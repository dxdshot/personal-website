Chat widget platform

Inbox tools like Intercom charge around $50 a month, and most of the paid features sat unused. My last role was part internship, part job, part project, depending on who you ask, and I built a replacement in seven days.

The result is a chat widget that drops onto any site, plus the dashboard I answered from. The widget and dashboard cover the whole conversation: live messages, typing indicators, read receipts, attachments, visitor context like country and browser, tags and topics, canned replies, suggested questions, a welcome message, CSAT, and starting or closing a chat. The new setup replaced the paid inbox, and the bubble in the corner of this page is the same widget.
